The lineup

Two kinds of flash. Both hand made.

Spoons

A curved metal blade that wobbles and flashes like a wounded baitfish. Bright paint on one side, hammered silver on the other.

Spinners

A blade that spins around a wire shaft, throwing flash and a thump fish can feel. Hammered silver blade with a painted tip and body.

Give a lure a tap to set it swinging. Looking for a certain color? Ask Joey at the booth.

  • Quality

    Built by hand, one lure at a time. Nothing goes on the table until it looks right and runs right.

  • Durability

    Put together to handle hard casts, river rock and fish that fight back.

  • Results

    These are the same lures Joey ties on when he fishes the Kalama River.
